01What is Aureliar?

Aureliar is a private society for founders who refuse to live an average life. We don't just gather, we build: partnerships, deals, and a standard that pulls everyone up.

02Who is in Aureliar?

Founders, mostly. Ecommerce, agencies, SaaS, creators, models, and everything around them. What they share is one thing: ambition.

03What does it cost?

Nothing. For now.

04What happens if I'm accepted?

You'll receive an email. After that, we set up your profile, you're whitelisted into the private groups and you meet everyone. Your level starts climbing from there.

05What happens if I'm rejected?

You'll hear from us by email. Most no's come down to timing, when we're near capacity, or an application that didn't show enough yet. Wait a few months, work and apply again.

06Why should I join Aureliar?

We aren't here to convince you. If you belong here, you already know it.
